# Nearby Bus Times

## Dublin, Ireland | TFI Bus Services

**Nearby Bus Times is a live bus departure app designed specifically for TFI bus services operating in Dublin, Ireland.**

Find nearby stops, view upcoming departures, search for stops across the supported Dublin network, and save your favourite stops for quick access.

## Features

- Find TFI bus stops near your current location
- View live realtime bus departures
- Automatically fall back to scheduled times when realtime predictions aren't available
- Search for **any supported bus stop in the Dublin system**
- Save favourite bus stops
- View favourite stops even when you're not physically nearby
- Clearly identify **LIVE** and **SCHEDULED** departures
- Automatic departure updates

## Home-Screen Widget

Nearby Bus Times includes an Android home-screen widget designed to give you quick access to the next buses without opening the app.

The widget:

- Automatically displays the **nearest bus stop**
- Shows the next upcoming departures
- Displays the route number and destination
- Identifies **LIVE** realtime departures
- Falls back to scheduled departure information when realtime data isn't available
- Includes a refresh button
- Opens the Nearby Bus Times app when tapped

The widget is designed around one simple question:

> **What bus is coming near me?**

No configuration or favourite-stop selection is required. The widget is focused on the nearest available stop.

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer
- Internet connection
- Location permission for nearby-stop functionality

## Coverage

**Current coverage: TFI bus services in Dublin, Ireland**

Nearby Bus Times currently focuses exclusively on supported **TFI bus services and bus stops in the Dublin area**.

Coverage of other TFI services or locations may be considered in future versions.
